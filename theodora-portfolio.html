What the debugging taught me

Order matters in cleaning

Duplicate rows stayed hidden until whitespace was stripped first — the sequence of cleaning steps changes what you actually find.

Accuracy can lie

A churn model can hit 86% accuracy while catching almost none of the customers who actually leave. Recall told the real story.

More features, not more magic

Regression accuracy nearly doubled just by giving the model thirteen relevant inputs instead of one.

Tuning doesn't always move the needle

Grid search on the Random Forest confirmed the defaults were already close to optimal — a valid, honest result in itself.
